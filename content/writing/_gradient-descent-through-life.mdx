## Ideas to pull on

- You only ever see the local slope. You never see the whole landscape, and you don't need to.
- Learning rate: steps too big overshoot and oscillate, steps too small never get anywhere.
- Local minima and saddle points: feeling stuck versus actually being done.
- Noise (SGD) as a feature. Randomness is what knocks you out of a shallow basin.
- Momentum: habits carry you through flat stretches.
- The loss function is a choice. What are you actually minimizing?
- Annealing: explore early, settle later. A link to the Ising post and temperature?
